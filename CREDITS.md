The painted backgrounds, oval miniature, side borders, vintage cars, ornate frame and night palace are taken from the purchased Missing Piece Invites artwork (cleaned of sample names, dates, watermarks and template branding). Live typography is HTML, so wording can be changed in `config.js`. Source images were low-resolution captures, so the pictures will not look as sharp as a print file.
